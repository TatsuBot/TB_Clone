Boss strategy notes (from paste, condensed):
  - Guard creates stone shields; does not trigger supporting fire from other bosses.
  - Soldier (laser / Grunt boss) gets supporting fire from Guard.
  - Airsoldier (lateral / Airgrunt boss) gets supporting fire from Soldier.
  - Guard pattern: turn1 lateral 2-space shields both sides → turn2 shields drop +
    cross attack → turn3 shields again perpendicular to previous. Pincer laterally
    the turn before vertical shields to avoid being shoved.
  - Priority: clear small fry + Guard first, then Soldier (stay off laser lines),
    then Airsoldier (avoid sticking to sides).
